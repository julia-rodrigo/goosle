# Working on Goosle

## How we work (always)

1. **Interview first.** Before designing or building anything new, ask the owner questions about it and wait for their answers.
2. **Show for approval.** Then show images (mockups, logo drafts, screenshots) and describe the functionality. Wait for the owner to approve both.
3. **Build only after approval.** Don't build or send the app (APK) until the images and functionality are approved.

This applies to every new feature, redesign and logo change, not only the first one.

## Project

Goosle is an Android app (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose) that makes social media calmer:
hides Shorts/Reels and AI slop inside YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, blocks apps,
breaks and daily limits, weekly focus topics, and a phone-wide ad blocker.
See README.md for details. Build with `./gradlew assembleRelease`; tests with `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest`.

Visual style: black and white, simple.
